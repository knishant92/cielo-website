Your questions

The eight things brands ask before a creative brief.

01Show us some references.

The gallery at the top of this page, filtered by type. More by category on request, including your own category.

02What adapts and marketplaces are covered for A+, RPDs and banners?

Amazon A+ basic and premium, Flipkart RPD, PDP infographics for Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa, Shopify and D2C, brand stores on Amazon, Myntra and Flipkart, and banners for every marketplace placement, website and quick commerce. Every size each channel currently asks for.

03What assets and formats are covered?

Static and animated banners, A+ and RPD modules, gallery infographics, store pages, films and reels in 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9, social statics, emailers and ad sets. Delivered in the marketplace's spec and naming.

04What creatives do you design?

A+ and RPD, PDP infographics, brand stores, banners, campaign films and reels, and marketing collaterals. The table above lists each with where it runs.

05What product categories have you worked in?

Fashion and innerwear, electronics and wearables, luggage, FMCG and nutraceuticals, beauty and personal care, footwear, home and fitness, kidswear, watches and jewellery, food and beverage, across marketplaces, lifestyle and retail.

06What do you need from our side?

Product images, brand guidelines or your best existing creative, the copy points per SKU, and where it will run. If you have no product images, the AI shoot makes them first.

07What is the process and timelines?

Brief, Plan, Create, QC, Deliver, Scale. Timelines per deliverable are in the table above, agreed at step 02 for your batch.

08What is the turnaround on A+, RPDs, infographics, website banners and Myntra store banners?

Per the table above. Post production runs at 3 to 5 days standard. Films are set per storyboard.
