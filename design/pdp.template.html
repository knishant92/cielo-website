Your live pages scored against the category leaders on the same marketplace.

  • Gallery: angle count, on-model, spec compliance, zoom
  • Copy: title, bullets, keywords, claims, character limits
  • A+ or RPD: present, complete, on brand
  • Video, size chart, fit notes, buyer questions
  • Compliance and rejection risks
  • Gap versus the top three in the category

All three services on one brief, so the page lands together, not in pieces.

Your questions

What Brands Ask About PDPs.

Asked by prospects in our own calls, answered here in plain words.

01How do the timelines work?

The audit comes first and sets the batch. Then the AI shoot, the listing copy and the creatives run in parallel and land on the page together. Shoot runs at 2 to 3 days per 100 SKUs, listings at 4 to 7 days per batch, post production at 3 to 5 days, films per storyboard. The end-to-end figure for your batch is set in the pilot plan.

02What is covered in PDP services?

Three offers: the audit, the revamp, and marketplace PDP content. Between them, everything on the page: gallery, title and bullets, size chart and fit notes, product film, A+ or RPD and infographics, buyer questions, upload and go-live.

03What are the commercials?

Per SKU, by scope: audit, revamp, or adapt per marketplace. Quoted with the pilot plan once the audit has sized the batch.

04What's covered in a PDP audit?

Gallery, copy, A+ or RPD, video, size chart and fit notes, buyer questions, compliance and rejection risks, each scored per SKU against the top three in your category on the same marketplace. You get the score, a fix list in priority order and a batch plan.

05What's covered in a PDP revamp?

A new gallery from the AI shoot, rewritten title, bullets and description, A+ or RPD and infographics, a product film, size chart, fit notes and buyer Q&A, all uploaded and tracked until live. One brief, one timeline.

06What's covered in marketplace PDP content?

The same product adapted to each platform's modules, image slots, attribute depth and rules: Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, Nykaa, your own store and quick commerce, from one master set.
