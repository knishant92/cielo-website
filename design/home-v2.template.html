What changes for your brand

Four things you get. Everything we do sits under one of them.

01 · SPEED TO SHELF

Live sooner. Selling for longer.

A style that goes live two weeks late has two fewer weeks to sell before the season turns. We take out the two slow parts: waiting for a studio, and listings bouncing back from the marketplace.

On-model images from a flatlay or mannequin, no studio bookingListings written to each marketplace's rules, so they pass first timeBatches, not one-offs, so the whole drop lands together

Amazon.in suppresses listings whose main image misses its spec, and Myntra will not accept a flatlay as a primary apparel image. Amazon Seller Central

02 · CONVERSION

PDPs that answer the buyer.

Shoppers go to the images first. Then they look for the angle, the fabric, the fit and the one line that settles their doubt. A PDP that answers all of that sells. One that makes them guess does not.

Five angles per SKU, on a model, every timeA+ and infographics that carry the reasons to buyTitles, bullets and descriptions written for the buyer and the search box

56% of shoppers look at product images before the title or reviews. Baymard Institute

03 · RETURNS

Photos that match the parcel.

A return that says "looked different online" is a content failure, and it costs you twice: the shipping and the sale. Our QC checks the print, the colour, the fit and the size chart against the real garment before anything ships.

Human sign-off on every AI-generated assetPrints, motifs and logos kept true to the inputSize charts and fit notes built into the page

Online apparel in India returned at 24.4% in 2023, the highest of any category. Unicommerce

04 · SCALE

Every SKU, every marketplace. Nothing left unlisted.

The styles that never got shot are the styles that never sold. We produce for the full catalogue, then adapt each asset to Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, Nykaa and your own store.

How we work

AI every day. A person on every asset.

AI is not a feature we sell you. It is how the team works, on every job, every day. That is where the speed comes from. The judgement stays human: someone looks at every image, every bullet, every banner before it reaches you.

  1. 09:30
    Brief lands

    Your SKUs, packshots and guidelines come in. A producer sets the batch, formats and the marketplaces it has to pass.

  2. 10:00
    First variants AI

    On-model angles, copy drafts and layout options are generated for the whole batch, not one SKU at a time.

  3. 12:00
    Select and fix PEOPLE

    Stylists and editors pick, correct prints and fit, rewrite what reads like a machine wrote it.

  4. 15:00
    QC against the spec PEOPLE

    Every asset checked against the garment and the marketplace's rules. What fails goes back a step, not to you.

  5. 17:30
    Delivered, named, sized

    Channel-ready files in the naming and dimensions each marketplace wants. Ready to upload, or we upload.
