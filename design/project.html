Approach

Flatlay In. On-Model Out, Every Angle.

01 · BRIEF AND PLAN

Moodboard and Model Brief

Age, body and styling matched to the range. One look agreed before generation, so the drop reads as one shoot.

02 · CREATE · AI ASSISTED SPEED

Required Angles, Every SKU

Front, back, side, detail and full length from each flatlay, the same set for every SKU in the batch.

03 · QC · HUMAN ASSURED QUALITY

Print and Fit Checked

Every frame checked against the flatlay for print placement, colour and fit. Drifts went back to generation, not to the brand.

04 · DELIVER

Listed and Live

Files at each marketplace's spec and naming, titles and bullets written, uploaded and tracked until live.
