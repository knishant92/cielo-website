Your questions

What Brands Ask Before a Listing Batch.

Asked by prospects in our own calls, answered here in plain words.

01Do you also upload A+ and RPDs?

Yes. Built by our creatives team, uploaded by our listings team, mapped to the right ASINs and FSNs.

02Do you also upload store banners for Amazon and Myntra stores?

Yes, both. Designed, sized to each store's placements and placed live.

03Do you write the copies: marketplace-compliant titles, descriptions, bullets?

Yes. Keyword-led, within each marketplace's character limits and claim rules, edited by people after AI drafts the batch.

04What marketplaces, channels and platforms do you list on?

Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, Nykaa, Tata CLiQ and Tata CLiQ Luxury, JioMart, Shopify and quick commerce. Any other marketplace you sell on, with its rulebook.

05What are the charges per option?

Priced per SKU by marketplace and package: new listing, enrichment or revamp. Quoted with the pilot plan.

06What do you need from our side?

A product data sheet, product images or the go-ahead for an AI shoot, brand notes and USPs, and marketplace access or a flat-file handover.

07What is covered in the complete package?

Keyword research, copy, attributes and structure, images to spec, upload, A+ and RPD upload, store banners, audit and revamp, QC and go-live tracking. The package section above lists each.

08What is the turnaround time for a batch of 100 SKUs?

4 to 7 days on average, from data in to live on one marketplace. Additional marketplaces run in parallel from the same data.

09What's included, and who uploads the images?

Everything in the package section, and we upload the images along with the listing, sized and named to spec.
