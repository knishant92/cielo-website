Your questions

The sixteen things brands ask before an AI shoot.

Asked by prospects in our own calls, answered here in plain words.

01Do you also produce lifestyle shoots using AI?

Yes. Indoor and outdoor scenes from the same flatlay or mannequin input, matched to the PDP stills.

02Do you also produce outdoor campaign shoots using AI?

Yes. Pitch, street, beach, city: the setting is part of the moodboard we agree before generation.

03How many angles do you provide in the final folder?

The required angles for each marketplace, the same set for every SKU in the batch. Typically front, back, side, detail and full length, in the order the marketplace wants them.

04What about character, voice, product consistency?

One model, face and styling across a range so the PDPs read as one shoot. Product consistency across angles and frames is checked in QC against the input. Voice, for video, follows the script you approve.

05Do prints, patterns and motifs match the actual garment?

They are checked frame by frame against the input: placement, scale and colour. A frame where the print drifts goes back to generation, not to you.

06What are the commercials per video?

Priced per video by length and format, and quoted with the pilot plan once the storyboard is agreed.

07What do you need as input?

Product images or packshots (flatlay, mannequin or ghost, front and back), brand guidelines if any, the SKU list with sizes and colourways, and the marketplaces you sell on.

08What is the duration of the video?

6 to 30 seconds, cut per channel. Longer explainers on request.

09What is the process?

Brief, Plan, Create, QC, Deliver, Scale. AI does the volume in Create. People select, fix and QC before Deliver.

10What is the speed, output per day?

At full run, 300 SKUs a day with all required angles, and 10 videos a day.

11What is the turnaround time of delivery?

2 to 3 days for a batch of 100 SKUs, from brief to files. Video turnaround is set per film when the storyboard is approved.

12What resolutions and formats do you provide the videos in?

MP4 at 1080p, in 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9 from one master. Other ratios on request.

13What types of videos do you create?

AI reels, AI explainer videos, AI product films, AI ecommerce videos, AI stylized videos and AI marketing videos.

14What will be required from our side?

Product images or packshots, brand guidelines if any, the SKU list, and your marketplaces. No shoot day, studio or model casting.

15What's included: moodboards, directions, script, storyboard?

Moodboard and shot direction, model and styling brief, and for video the script and storyboard, all shared for approval before generation.

16Will product consistency be maintained?

Yes. Same garment, print, colour and fit across every angle and frame, checked against the input in QC.
