Your questions

What Brands Ask Before a Post Batch.

The questions a post batch raises, answered in plain words.

01Can we send our own shoot, without using your AI shoot or creatives?

Yes. Post production runs under our own shoots and on its own. Send the raw files and the spec and it goes through the same queue.

02What is the turnaround?

3 to 5 days standard for images. Faster lanes are agreed per batch when a launch needs it. Video is set per film at the brief.

03How much volume can you take?

The studio finishes 1.5M+ images a month. For a retailer we set up a standing lane sized to your daily drop, with headroom for sale-season peaks.

04Do you follow marketplace image rules?

Yes. Each marketplace's background, size, margin and naming rules are applied per channel, and checked in QC before delivery.

05What formats do you take and return?

In: JPG, RAW, TIFF, PSD, and for video MP4, MOV or ProRes. Out: JPG or PNG to spec, layered PSD on request, MP4 at 1080p per channel.

06How do we send files?

A shared drive, FTP or your DAM. Transfer and storage are access-controlled under our ISO 27001 process.

07What if a frame comes back wrong?

It comes back to us. Rework against the agreed style guide is part of the batch, not a new job.

08What are the commercials?

Per image by job type and volume, per video by length and cuts. Quoted with the pilot plan after a test batch.
